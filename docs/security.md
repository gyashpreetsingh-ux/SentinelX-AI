## 1. Safety Guardrails & Zero Real-World Impact
SentinelX AI is strictly a **defensive cyber operations** and **educational simulation** platform. Under no circumstances does SentinelX AI execute offensive operations or alter actual host machines.

### What SentinelX AI Does NOT Do:
- Does NOT scan external or unauthorized networks.
- Does NOT collect real passwords or credentials.
- Does NOT execute destructive payloads or wiper routines.
- Does NOT retaliate or launch counter-attacks against external IP addresses.
- Does NOT modify host operating system network adapters or actual production disks.

### What SentinelX AI DOES:
- Operates on virtual simulated endpoint entities (`Endpoint-01` through `Endpoint-08`).
- Generates safe synthetic telemetry and logs.
- Simulates network isolation and quarantine as internal application states.
- Simulates snapshot rollbacks and integrity verification via cryptographic checksums.
